How the pay works

The basics

$50 for every qualified held appointment. The prospect has to actually show up, and Kathleen approves it as qualified after the call. Booked but not held earns nothing, and each prospect counts once.

Monthly volume bonus

On top of the $50s, based on qualified held appointments in a single calendar month. Only the highest tier you reach is paid, and the tiers don't stack.

  • New, added Aug 25: 10 to 14 in a month: +$150, so 12 calls pays $750
  • 15 to 19 in a month: +$250, so 15 calls pays $1,000
  • 20 to 25 in a month: +$400, so 20 calls pays $1,400
  • 26 to 33 in a month: +$600, so 26 calls pays $1,900
  • 34 or more in a month: +$1,000, so 34 calls pays $2,700

The count resets on the first of every month.

What makes a call qualified

  • The prospect actually attended. No-shows don't count, and a reschedule counts once it's held.
  • It's a real business prospect, not a vendor, job seeker, agency partner, or someone already in our pipeline.
  • They fit our ideal client, and the person on the call can make or influence the buying decision.
  • They have a real need we can solve, and a realistic ability to pay our $3,000/month minimum. Kathleen can approve exceptions below that in writing, ahead of the call.
  • You asked about budget before booking, and the notes were complete before the call: source, company, contact, role, the need, budget signal, timeline, and why you think it qualifies.

Getting paid

Twice a month, on the schedule above. Send an invoice for each period with a short summary of the calls you're claiming.
